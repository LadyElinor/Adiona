# Security policy

Adiona 0.1 is a sandbox research seed. No production-safe release is declared.
Do not supply real secrets, personal records, high-impact decisions or actuator
credentials to its examples.

For suspected issues, prepare the smallest local reproduction, identify the
commit and affected claim, and describe the assumed attacker access. Separate
an ordinary malformed-request rejection from an accepted unauthorized transition.
Do not test against systems you do not control.

Do not place credentials or live exploit material in a public issue. Use a
maintainer-established private reporting channel. Private vulnerability reporting
has not been configured or verified by this initialization.

Reports that undermine an advertised gate suspend that claim until independently
reviewed and repaired. Preserve failing examples and historical results; do not
rewrite the old benchmark to make the defect disappear.
